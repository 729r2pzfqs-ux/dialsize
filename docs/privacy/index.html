Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information We Collect

DialSize.com does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information. We use two Google services to understand how the site is used and to fund it through advertising:

  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed, time on site, device type, and general geographic region. We do not use Google Analytics to collect information that directly identifies you.
  • Google AdSense: Displays advertisements on the site. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

DialSize.com does not set its own tracking cookies. Analytics and advertising cookies are set by Google.

Cookie Consent

Visitors in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland: Before any analytics or advertising cookies are set, you will see a consent message provided by Google. Analytics and personalized advertising are only enabled if you choose to allow them. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which use only limited data such as general location and the page being viewed.

Visitors in all other regions: Analytics and personalized advertising are enabled by default. You can opt out at any time using the options below.

Managing Your Preferences

  • Control personalized advertising from Google at Google Ad Settings.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics on all websites with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, clearing cookies for this site will show Google's consent message again on your next visit so you can change your choice.

Affiliate Links

Some links on DialSize.com are affiliate links. When you click these links and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This helps us maintain and improve the site. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Data Retention

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months. We do not store any personal information beyond what is collected by Google Analytics and Google AdSense.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at info@dialsize.com.
