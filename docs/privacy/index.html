Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information We Collect

DialSize.com collects minimal information to operate and improve our service:

  • Analytics data: We use Google Analytics 4 with Consent Mode v2 to understand how visitors use our site. When you consent, this includes page views, time on site, and general geographic region. No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Cookies: We use essential cookies for site functionality and, with your consent, analytics and advertising cookies.

Google AdSense

We display ads through Google AdSense. When you consent to advertising cookies, Google may use information about your visits to this and other websites to provide relevant advertisements. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings.

Affiliate Links

Some links on DialSize.com are affiliate links. When you click these links and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This helps us maintain and improve the site. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.

Your Rights

You can reject non-essential cookies using our cookie banner. You can also use your browser settings to block cookies or clear existing ones. Under GDPR and similar regulations, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained for 14 months. We do not store any personal information beyond what Google Analytics collects with your consent.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at info@dialsize.com.
