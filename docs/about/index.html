About DialSize

What is DialSize?

DialSize is a comprehensive watch dimensions database built for watch enthusiasts, buyers, and researchers. We catalog the exact measurements of hundreds of watches so you can compare sizes, check wrist fit, and make informed purchasing decisions — all without visiting a boutique.

Why Dimensions Matter

Watch brands advertise diameter, but that number alone does not tell you how a watch wears. Lug-to-lug distance determines whether a watch overhangs your wrist. Thickness determines how it sits under a shirt cuff. Lug width determines strap compatibility. We track all of these.

Our Data

All dimensions are sourced from manufacturer specifications, verified against multiple independent sources, and regularly updated. Where manufacturer data is unavailable, we note the source. Each watch entry includes the date it was last verified.

How to Use DialSize

  • Search: Use the search bar to find any watch by brand, model, or reference number.
  • Compare: Our comparison pages put two watches side by side with differences highlighted.
  • Guides: Browse our curated guides for specific needs — small wrists, thin watches, particular lug widths.
  • Wrist Fit: Each watch page includes a wrist fit estimate based on lug-to-lug distance.
