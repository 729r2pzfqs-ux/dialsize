About DialSize

What is DialSize?

DialSize is a watch dimensions database built for watch enthusiasts, buyers, and researchers. We catalog the measurements of watches from major brands so you can compare sizes, check wrist fit, and make informed purchasing decisions — all without visiting a boutique.

Why Dimensions Matter

Watch brands advertise diameter, but that number alone does not tell you how a watch wears. Lug-to-lug distance determines whether a watch overhangs your wrist. Thickness determines how it sits under a shirt cuff. Lug width determines strap compatibility. We track all of these.

Our Data

Dimensions are taken from manufacturer specifications. Each watch page lists diameter, lug-to-lug, thickness, and lug width, along with case, movement, and water resistance details where the manufacturer publishes them. If a measurement is not published, we show it as unavailable rather than estimating it.

Spotted an error or have a correction? Let us know using the contact details below and we will update the entry.

How to Use DialSize

  • Search: Use the search bar to find any watch by brand, model, or reference number.
  • Compare: Our comparison pages put two watches side by side with differences highlighted.
  • Guides: Browse our guides for specific needs — small wrists, thin watches, particular sizes.
  • Lug Widths: Find every watch that fits a given strap size on our lug width pages.
  • Wrist Fit: Each watch page includes a wrist fit estimate based on lug-to-lug distance.
