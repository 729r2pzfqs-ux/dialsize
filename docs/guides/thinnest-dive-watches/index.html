Thinnest Dive Watches

A thick dive watch is one of the main reasons people leave their diver in the drawer. These models deliver at least 200 m of water resistance while keeping the case profile as slim as possible.

Why Thickness Matters for Dive Watches

Traditional dive watches were built like tanks — 14-16 mm thick. Modern materials and engineering have brought that number down considerably. A thinner diver sits closer to the wrist, slides under shirt cuffs, and is more comfortable during all-day wear.

How We Measured

We include only watches with at least 200 m (20 ATM) water resistance, sorted by case thickness from thinnest to thickest. All measurements are manufacturer-stated specifications.
