Watches Under 47mm Lug-to-Lug

Lug-to-lug distance is the single most important dimension for wrist fit. Watches under 47 mm lug-to-lug will sit comfortably on wrists as small as 6 inches (15 cm) without overhanging.

Why Lug-to-Lug Matters More Than Diameter

A 42 mm watch with 46 mm lug-to-lug can wear smaller than a 38 mm watch with 49 mm lugs. The lug-to-lug measurement determines whether the watch "bridges" past the edges of your wrist — and that is the primary visual cue for whether a watch is too large.

The Rule of Thumb

Your lug-to-lug distance should ideally be less than the width of your wrist (measured across the top). For a 6-inch wrist, that is roughly 50 mm; for 6.5 inches, about 53 mm. Going under 47 mm gives comfortable margin for most people.
