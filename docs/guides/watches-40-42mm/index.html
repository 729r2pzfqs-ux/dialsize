Best Watches Between 40-42mm

The 40-42 mm range is the modern Goldilocks zone. Large enough for a strong wrist presence, small enough to fit most wrists comfortably. This is where the majority of popular sport and dive watches live.

Why 40-42mm?

This range works for wrists from about 6.25 inches to 8 inches — covering the vast majority of wearers. The dial is large enough for easy readability, complications have room to breathe, and the proportions look balanced on most arms.

The Most Popular Segment

The majority of iconic watch references fall in this range: the Rolex Submariner (41 mm), Omega Speedmaster (42 mm), Tudor Black Bay (41 mm), and Seiko Prospex divers (40.5-42.7 mm). If you are buying your first serious watch, this is likely where you will end up.
