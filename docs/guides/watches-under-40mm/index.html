Best Watches Under 40mm for Small Wrists

Not everyone wants a dinner-plate on their wrist. Watches under 40 mm wear elegantly on slimmer wrists and look proportionally balanced on just about anyone. This guide covers every watch in our database that comes in under that threshold, from luxury Swiss pieces to affordable Japanese movements.

Why Choose a Watch Under 40mm?

The trend toward ever-larger watches peaked in the early 2010s and has been reversing since. Many brands now recognize that 36-39 mm is the sweet spot for versatility. A sub-40 mm watch slips under a shirt cuff, looks refined at formal events, and is comfortable for all-day wear.

What to Look For

When shopping for a smaller watch, pay attention to these dimensions:

  • Lug-to-lug distance: More important than diameter for on-wrist fit. Under 46 mm is ideal for wrists under 6.5 inches.
  • Thickness: A compact diameter paired with a thick case can look awkward. Under 12 mm is ideal.
  • Lug width: Smaller watches typically use 18-20 mm straps, which are widely available.
